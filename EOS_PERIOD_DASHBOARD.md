# EOS Period Dashboard

## Purpose

Define the future management/month-end dashboard for EOS accounting periods.

- replace the old monthly Excel sheet browsing mindset
- provide a month-level control view
- support month-end review and period close readiness

## Main Sections

- period summary
- submitted vs not submitted reports
- discrepancy count
- unresolved manager reviews
- temporary closures pending review
- abandoned / superseded shifts
- period close status

## Suggested Filters

- business unit
- location
- department
- accounting period
- status

## Suggested KPIs / Cards

- total reports
- submitted reports
- draft / saved-only reports
- reports with discrepancies
- pending manager reviews
- emergency handovers
- unresolved temporary closures

## Suggested List / Detail Areas

- report list for selected period
- discrepancy list for selected period
- unresolved shift list
- period close readiness checklist

## Rule

- management / admin only
- separate from operational EOS
- separate from the discrepancy review page
